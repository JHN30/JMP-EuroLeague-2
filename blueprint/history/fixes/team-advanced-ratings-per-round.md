# Fix: Team Advanced ratings chart shows each round's own ratings

**Type:** Fix
**Status:** verified
**Branch:** fix/team-advanced-ratings-per-round

## The problem

On a team's Advanced tab the "Offense and defense through the season" chart plots the club's offensive and defensive rating as **season-to-date values**: the point for round N is the rating over all of the club's games up to round N (the trend route says so: "one point per round of cumulative values", and the rows' `gamesPlayed` counts up). So the lines are running averages: they start jumpy, settle as the season goes on and end on the season's average. They show how the average moved, not how the club played in a round, so a bad or a hot round is nearly invisible after a few weeks. The caption was reworded in 31h-iv to say "Season to date" because "cumulative" did not explain itself; the chart itself should show the rounds.

## The fix

Plot each round's own offensive and defensive rating, and keep the season values as reference.

- **Backend** (`GET /api/seasons/:season/teams/:club/advanced`, `backend/src/routes/seasons.ts`, a small pure helper next to the readers in `backend/src/db/season-advanced.ts`): every `trend` row gets four new fields, computed from the cumulative columns the rows already come from (`points_for`, `points_against`, `possessions`, `games_played` of `app_team_round_stats`, which are cumulative like the ratings): `roundOffensiveRating = (pointsFor - previous pointsFor) / (possessions - previous possessions) * 100`, `roundDefensiveRating` the same with `pointsAgainst`, `roundNetRating` their difference, and `roundGames` (`gamesPlayed - previous gamesPlayed`). The first row's previous values are zero. A round with no new possessions (a club that did not play it, or a postponed game) has `null` for the three ratings and `0` games. Nothing existing changes: the cumulative `offensiveRating`, `defensiveRating`, `netRating`, `gamesPlayed`, `pace` and the rest stay as they are, so the season average is still the last row's value.
- **Frontend** (`frontend/src/teams/TeamAdvancedSection.jsx`, `RatingsChart.jsx`): the two lines are the per-round ratings (rounds with `null` are gaps, the lines span them); two dashed horizontal lines in the same colours are the season averages (the last row's cumulative offensive and defensive rating); the shaded gap between the two per-round lines stays green where the round's offense was ahead and red where behind; the tooltip's footer shows the round's net rating and its games. Under the chart a short row names the season averages ("Season average: Offense 110.3, Defense 117.0, Net -6.7 over 38 games") and the caption says what the lines are ("Each point is that round's own rating, in points scored and allowed per 100 possessions; the dashed lines are the season averages."). The badge keeps showing the season net rating after the latest round (labelled "Season net"). The phone layout from 31h-iv (dots by number of rounds, legend padding, height, badge under the title) is kept; a round with a double game week (two games) is one point.
- Not changed: the Splits, Play by play and Lineups blocks, the scope strip, any other endpoint, the database, the cumulative fields.

## Build steps

- [x] **1. Per-round ratings in the trend, and the chart.** Add the helper and the four fields to the trend rows; add `frontend/e2e/api-team-advanced.spec.js`; draw the per-round lines, the dashed season lines, the averages row and the new caption; update the mocks and checks in `team-advanced-layout.spec.js`. **Done when:** for a live club (a full 2025 season and a 2026 season in progress) the API returns the new fields for every round, the per-round net is the per-round offense minus defense, `roundGames` adds up to the last row's `gamesPlayed`, and the games-weighted mean of the per-round offensive ratings (weights: the round's possessions, recovered from the cumulative rows in the spec) equals the last row's cumulative offensive rating within 0.05; the chart at 320px and 1024px shows two lines that move round to round (not the smoothed ones) and two dashed lines at the season averages, the averages row and caption are on the page, a null round leaves a gap, and `npm run build` (both apps), `npm run lint`, `api-team-advanced.spec.js`, `team-advanced-layout.spec.js`, `team-statistics-roster-layout.spec.js` and `responsive.spec.js` pass.

## Verify

`cd backend && npm run build`; `cd frontend && npm run lint && npm run build`; `npx playwright test e2e/api-team-advanced.spec.js e2e/team-advanced-layout.spec.js e2e/responsive.spec.js` (the backend must be running, which Playwright does itself). By hand: open a club's Advanced tab (2025 Regular season): the lines are jagged round to round, the dashed lines sit at the season averages, the row under the chart gives the same numbers, and the last round's per-round point is not the season average.

## Notes

- There is no backend test runner (`backend` has `build` and `dev` only), so the helper's maths is checked through the live API in a Playwright spec, as the other API contract specs here do. If the cumulative columns turn out not to be cumulative in some scope, stop and say so rather than guess: step 1 first compares the first two rounds of a live club by hand (round 1's per-round value must equal its cumulative value).
- As built: the trend rows also carry `roundPossessions` (the round's possessions), not in the first spec: the API spec needs it to weight the rounds when it adds them back up (a rating is points per possession, so the possession-weighted mean of the rounds' ratings is the cumulative rating exactly). The cumulative assumption held on the live data: `api-team-advanced.spec.js` passes for 2025 and 2026 over six clubs and every scope (the first round's own ratings equal its cumulative ones, `roundGames` adds up to the last `gamesPlayed`, the weighted means match the last cumulative ratings within 0.05, the net is offense minus defense, a round without possessions has null ratings and 0 games). The helper is `teamRoundOwnRatings` in `backend/src/db/season-advanced.ts`; the route is the only caller.
- The chart: the dashed averages and the Net badge's "Season net" label use the cumulative values after the last round; the legend lists only the two round lines; the tooltip lists only them too and its footer shows the round's net rating and, for a double game week, its games. `RatingsChart` carries `data-average-offense` and `data-average-defense` so the spec can read the lines (a canvas cannot be read otherwise). Rounds with null ratings are gaps the lines span (the spec mocks three and checks the chart still draws without a page error). A screenshot at 320px and 1024px with the full 2025 season was checked.
- In the whole-suite run one `phone-menu.spec.js` case (reduced motion at 390px) failed under load, the known menu flake.
